Most symbols below have one meaning throughout the book; the few with a second meaning in a particular chapter say so. The last column gives where each symbol is used. Temperatures appear in formulas in kelvin (K) and in prose in degrees Celsius (&deg;C). Fractions such as strength and extraction yield are written as decimals in formulas (0.0135) and as percentages in prose (1.35%).
